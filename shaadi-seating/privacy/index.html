Privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Shaadi Seating ("the game") is made by Suraj Manickam. This policy explains what the game collects, why, and the choices you have. Contact: surajimmanuel@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, stars, coins, album, settings and daily streak are saved only on your phone. We never see them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

What the game sends, and why

The game uses three Google services. Each one receives a little data from your phone:

  • Ads (Google AdMob). To show ads and count them, AdMob receives your phone's advertising ID, an approximate location worked out from your internet address, basic device information and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice under Settings, Privacy choices.
  • Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics). To learn which levels are too hard and where players stop, the game sends events about play: levels started, finished or left, stars, time taken, hints used, ads watched and purchases made. These come with an app instance ID and basic device information. They never include your name, contacts, messages, or anything you type.
  • Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics). When the game crashes or an error happens, it sends a report of where in the game it happened, with basic device information and an installation ID, so we can fix it.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. The game receives only a confirmation of what you bought, never your card or bank details.

Sharing your daily result

Your daily result is shared only when you tap Share, and only through the app you choose.

Who receives the data

Data sent to AdMob and Firebase is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and their terms for these services. We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone else.

How long it's kept

Analytics event data (what happened in the game) is kept for 2 months. Data linked to your app instance ID, such as which version of the game you play on, is kept for up to 14 months. Crash reports are kept for about 90 days. These follow Google's settings for these services.

Your choices

  • Change your ad consent under Settings, Privacy choices, where it applies to you.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings: Settings, Google, Ads.
  • Ask us to delete analytics data linked to your app instance ID by writing to surajimmanuel@gmail.com.
  • Uninstall the game to delete everything saved on your phone.

Children

The game is for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the game and at https://nightmonkey94.github.io/shaadi-seating/privacy/, with a new date at the top.
